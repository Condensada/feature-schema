October 2, 2026 – Feature Department Orientation

Time: 8:10–8:40 PM; adjourned at 8:45 PM

Platform: Google Meet

Presider: Anjelo, Feature Editor

Present: Aura, Anjelo, Elaine, Jason, Raya, and Sofia

Absent: Ele (internet connection issue)

Welcome and department introduction

The Feature Editor welcomed the members and congratulated newcomers Ele and Elaine. The department was introduced as a group of storytellers covering movies, books, and other literary works. Each feature carries two stories: the subject's and the writer's own.

Editorial Board

  • Editor-in-Chief: Leads and supervises the Board and edits articles before publication.
  • Internal Associate Editor: Edits articles for the Editor-in-Chief and handles outreach to school organizations.

Planner, workflow, and expectations

  • The Feature Planner on Google Sheets was demonstrated; members confirmed their assigned tasks and due dates.
  • Article workflow: Writer → Feature Editor for editing → Associate Editor.
  • Members should message the Feature Editor before a deadline if they are stuck or running late.

Monthly themes

The October-to-June themes were presented and are built around seasons and Philippine observances. Examples mentioned were Spooky Season in October, True Stories in November, Simbang Gabi in December, and Buwan ng Panitikan in April.

Extra feature content

Formats presented: Artworks Feature; Spotlights and Behind the Scenes; Personal Feature; and Adaptation Comparison.

Ideas and suggestions were contributed by Anjelo, Elaine, Jason, and Raya. Elaine suggested writing about the Resident Evil movie for this month. Songs can also be featured in digests.

Action items

  • Feature Editor: Finalize the content plan using members' input and update the Planner.
